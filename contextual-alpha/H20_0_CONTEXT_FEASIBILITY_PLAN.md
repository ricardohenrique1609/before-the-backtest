# H20.0 — Context Data Feasibility: Plan and Pre-Registration

**Status: FROZEN before the sample was drawn and before any context retrieval.**
Its SHA-256 is recorded inside `H20_0_SAMPLE_FREEZE.json`. Any later change is an amendment, filed as
a separate file, never an edit to this one.

**Date:** 2026-10-18 · **Repository HEAD:** `8e57bf1` · **Write zone:** `Contextual Alpha/` only.

---

## 1. Question

> Can a sufficiently complete, timestamped, reproducible and point-in-time historical public-context
> corpus be reconstructed for prospective historical prediction research?

H20.0 is a **data-substrate** experiment. It does not test returns, alpha, profitability, delisting
prediction, context predictive power, sentiment, causality or strategies. No outcome is used to
decide whether a source is useful.

Allowed verdicts (closed list): **PASS · PARTIAL PASS · DATA-GATE FAIL / UNTESTED · BLOCKED.**
A failure here is a statement about the data, never an economic REJECT.

---

## 2. Units

- **Decision timestamp** `t` — a UTC instant (minute precision).
- **Information set** `I(t)` — documents with `public_availability_time ≤ t`. Strict inequality is
  not used: a document available exactly at `t` is admissible; one at `t + 1 min` is not.
- **Windows** — `W7(t) = (t − 7 d, t]` (primary), `W30(t) = (t − 30 d, t]` (asset-specific secondary).
- **Asset-time unit** `(t, a)` — asset `a` in the PIT Binance spot universe at `t`.
- **Document** — one record of the data model in `CONTEXT_DATA_MODEL.md`.

---

## 3. Sample (rule frozen here; generated by `scripts/h20_0_sample_freeze.py`)

**Timestamps.** One decision timestamp per calendar quarter, 2018-Q1 through 2026-Q3 (35 quarters).
Within each quarter, an instant drawn uniformly at minute resolution with
`numpy.random.default_rng(20261018)`, in quarter order. The period is fixed by calendar only. No
date is chosen, moved, added or dropped for any reason connected to market events. 2026-Q4 is
excluded because it is not complete at the freeze date.

**Assets.** For each `t`, the PIT universe `A(t)` is every base asset with at least one Binance spot
symbol whose window satisfies `first_candle ≤ t − 30 d` and `last_candle ≥ t` (inherited A5 snapshot;
the only function of `last_candle` used is this "trading at `t`" predicate). Each asset's liquidity
at `t` is the median daily `usd_volume` over `[t − 30 d, t)` (inherited A8; rows with `date < t`
only). Assets with no volume row in that window are excluded from stratification and counted. `A(t)`
is split into terciles of that liquidity; **one asset is drawn uniformly from each tercile** with the
same RNG stream. 35 × 3 = **105 asset-time units**.

**Freeze.** The freeze file is written and hashed before any context source is contacted for a
sample timestamp. Once frozen, no timestamp or asset enters or leaves — including for errors that are
later found to be the researcher's own (H18.0b convention).

## 4. Sources

Every source considered is entered in `registries/CONTEXT_SOURCE_REGISTRY.csv`, including those
rejected without probing; the registry records whether each assessment is **PROBED_H20**,
**INHERITED_EVIDENCE** or **DESK_ONLY**. Sources are probed against the frozen sample with fixed,
outcome-free query rules (`CONTEXT_RETRIEVAL_GOVERNANCE.md`):

| layer | probed sources (planned) |
|---|---|
| C1 macro | Federal Reserve press-release feed; ALFRED vintage data; Wikipedia Current-Events portal (revision as of `t`) |
| C2 political / regulatory | Federal Register API (fixed crypto term list); SEC, CFTC, DOJ press-release surfaces; Wikipedia Current-Events portal |
| C3 crypto systemic | GDELT 2.0 GKG slice at `t` (crypto themes); Wikipedia Current-Events portal |
| C4 exchange-specific | Binance CMS full catalogue (re-harvested, coverage-guarded); Binance article detail for documents in `W7` |
| C5 asset-specific | Binance CMS notices naming the asset; Wikipedia article for the PIT-resolved asset name; GDELT slice name match |
| archive layer | Internet Archive Wayback CDX (captures ≤ `t`) |

Collection is capped at what is needed to measure feasibility: no source is harvested beyond the
sample windows except the Binance CMS title catalogue, whose completeness is itself a measured
quantity and which is small (~10⁴ titles).

## 5. Measured quantities

All are computed by `scripts/h20_0_audit.py` from the collected tables.

1. **Coverage** — per source and layer: share of sample timestamps at which the source existed and
   was retrievable; documents per `W7` by layer and year; C4 and C5 coverage per asset-time unit.
2. **Timestamp quality** — shares of T0–T4 (`CONTEXT_TIMESTAMP_SCHEMA.md`) by source, layer, year.
3. **Version quality** — shares of V0–V5 (`CONTEXT_TIMESTAMP_SCHEMA.md` §5) by source, layer, year.
4. **Entity resolution** — PIT name resolution success / ambiguity / failure rates; ticker-era conflict
   rate (a mapping made with today's data would differ from the as-of-`t` mapping).
5. **Reproducibility** — refetch success and content-hash stability on a refetch subsample; archive
   persistence (capture exists ≤ `t`, capture exists at all).
6. **Missingness** — by year, source, layer, entity, asset age (days since first candle) and liquidity
   tercile. Missing (source absent/unretrievable) is kept distinct from zero (source present, nothing
   published).
7. **Bias audits** — temporal coverage, entity popularity, language, archive, timestamp, and source
   survivorship (the last one in the sealed stage of §7).

## 6. Gate (criteria frozen here, before any measurement)

These are **feasibility** criteria for H20.0. They are not the eligibility thresholds a future model
would apply to individual documents; those are deliberately left open until the distributions below
have been measured (`CONTEXT_TIMESTAMP_SCHEMA.md` §6).

A context layer **L ∈ {C1…C5}** is evaluated on its best probed source(s):

| # | criterion | bar |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | coverage — share of sample timestamps at which ≥1 probed source of L was available **and** retrievable | ≥ 90% |
| G2 | year span — calendar years 2018–2026 in which L has ≥1 retrievable document across the sample | ≥ 8 of 9 |
| G3 | timestamp quality — share of L's documents classed T0, T1 or T2 | ≥ 80%, **and** T4 ≤ 5% |
| G4 | version quality — share of L's documents classed V0, V1 or V2 (immutable, archived ≤ t, or versioned) | ≥ 70% |
| G5 | reproducibility — refetch success **and** hash stability on the refetch subsample | each ≥ 95% |
| G6 | licensing — derived-feature research use permitted for the source(s) carrying G1 | yes |
| G7 | entity resolution (C4, C5 only) — PIT resolution success | ≥ 80%, ambiguous ≤ 10% |

**Severe-bias downgrade.** A layer that passes G1–G7 is downgraded to *CONDITIONAL* if any bias audit
for it is severe: a coverage ratio ≥ 3× or a gap ≥ 30 pp between liquidity terciles, between the
earliest and latest three sample years, or between still-listed and no-longer-listed assets.

**Verdict.**
- **PASS** — all five layers pass with no CONDITIONAL.
- **PARTIAL PASS** — at least one layer passes (with or without CONDITIONAL), and the failing layers
  are named with the criterion that failed.
- **DATA-GATE FAIL / UNTESTED** — no layer passes.
- **BLOCKED** — the measurement could not be run (access, tooling) for reasons other than the data.

A criterion that could not be measured is **not passed** (H18.0 convention: an untested criterion is
not a satisfied one).

## 7. Outcome blindness and the sealed survivorship stage

- Collection and feature-free coverage measurement read **no** outcome table (inheritance decisions
  A3, A4, A9). A test fails the build if any H20 script other than the sealed stage references those
  paths.
- §19's *source survivorship* question — do documents disappear disproportionately for entities that
  were later removed? — needs a lifecycle flag, which is an outcome. It is therefore run as a **sealed
  final stage** (`scripts/h20_0_sealed_survivorship.py`) that refuses to run unless
  `CONTEXT_EVIDENCE_MANIFEST.csv` and the collected tables have already been hashed into
  `manifests/H20_0_PRE_SEAL_HASHES.json`. It joins only one coarse flag (any Binance spot route live
  at the 2026-10 harvest: yes/no, inherited A6) and only to compute coverage differences. Nothing it
  produces can feed back into sampling, source choice or collection, because those are finished and
  hashed before it runs.
- No return, price change, volatility or post-`t` market quantity is computed anywhere in H20.0.

## 8. Stop rule

At the end of H20.0 the project stops and reports. H20.1 (representation feasibility) is not begun,
even on a PASS.
