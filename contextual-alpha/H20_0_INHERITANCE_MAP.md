**The most useful inheritance is not a dataset but a fact:** H18.0b/H18.1b showed that Binance's
official CMS (`bapi/composite/v1/public/cms/article/list/query`) serves its whole announcement history
since 2017-07 with millisecond `releaseDate` stamps, keyless, and that its catalogue totals can be
checked against the API's own reported count. That makes it the obvious first C4 candidate. The H18
**tables** built on it, by contrast, are almost all selected on delisting vocabulary or carry outcome
columns, and are excluded from H20.0 by §5 (outcome blindness).

**Two inherited defects carried forward as risks, not fixed in place:**

1. `data/raw/h18_1b/catalog_titles.parquet` holds **3,600** catalog-49 titles against the **4,442**
   that `data/alpha_sprint/h18_1b_discover.json` records as harvested from the same catalogue, and its
   oldest catalog-49 title is 2020-12-21 against the discover run's 2017 floor. 3,600 is exactly 72
   full pages of 50. `scripts/h18_1b_rebrand.py` ends a catalogue on the first empty page
   (`if not a: break`) — the same pagination defect H18.1b §3.5 found and fixed in
   `h18_1b_discover.py`, but not in this second harvester — and also de-duplicates across catalogues,
   which could explain part of the gap. *Suspected at audit time; H20.0 then re-harvested the
   catalogue with a coverage guard.* **CONFIRMED (NEW H20 EVIDENCE, 2026-10-18):** the 842 missing
   articles are all catalog 49, all 2017-07 → 2020-12, and none is a cross-catalogue duplicate — a
   pagination truncation, not de-duplication (`data/interim/binance_snapshot_diff.json`; report §1).
   The H18 file is not altered; any H18 result that read it is outside H20's remit and is flagged for
   the sponsor.
2. Symbol windows are reconstructed from Binance's **current** archive listing. H18.1b §4.2 showed
   that an asset whose ticker was later reused (Skycoin/`SKY`, StableUSD/`USDS`) leaves **no pairs in
   the archive at all**. A PIT asset universe built from these windows therefore under-represents
   some early, later-removed assets. H20.0 inherits this as a known survivorship limitation of the
   entity universe, not of the context sources.

## 2. Inherited evidence vs new H20 evidence

Everything in this file is **INHERITED EVIDENCE** with its source path. It enters H20 only as (a) a
risk to test, (b) an identity or PIT rule, or (c) a method. No H18/H19 measurement is reported as an
H20 result. Where H20 re-measures something H18 already measured (catalogue completeness, timestamp
precision), the H20 number is reported as **NEW H20 EVIDENCE** and the H18 number beside it, labelled.
