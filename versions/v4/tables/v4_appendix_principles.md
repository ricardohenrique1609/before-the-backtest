# Appendix E table — Principles not carried in the body

Four principles are carried in the body of V3 (P1–P4). Six more were stated in earlier versions and
are retained here, with the status marking introduced in V2: `GENERAL`, or `CONDITIONAL` with the
condition named. They are supporting observations, not contributions.

| # | principle | status | condition | established by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | The identifier is the experiment: identity is (entity, era), never a reusable symbol alone. | `GENERAL` | — | a ticker-keyed join substituted an asset that acquired the symbol four years after the event |
| A2 | A date is not a datum until it is attributed to a decision by an entity at a time. | `GENERAL` | — | announcement, stated cessation and measured cessation disagree for three events |
| A3 | Every join needs its own outcome-differential audit. | `GENERAL` | — | identity passed at 98–99% while price coverage differed by 78.0 pp between entities with and without a current ticker |
| A4 | Who owns the record determines whether removed entities remain observable. | `CONDITIONAL` | research without paid data; for a funded desk the dependence moves to a vendor whose construction choices are less inspectable | a 0.0 pp venue retrieval differential against the aggregator-side bias documented in the delisting literature |
| A5 | A served candle is not a traded market. | `CONDITIONAL` | illiquid instruments and bar-level data; with trade-level data and an order book the distinction is already visible | spot has no trades in a median 25% of primary-window minutes against 0.42% for the perpetual |
| A6 | A positive development result is not evidence until a frozen out-of-sample evaluation survives. | `GENERAL` | — | 8 of 8 development folds positive, holdout reversed sign |

**Why these are not in the body.** V3 carries one contribution. A1–A3 and A6 are standard good
practice for which this program supplies instances rather than arguments; A4 and A5 are conditional
on the data environment. Keeping ten principles at equal prominence in the body was a structural
defect of V2, identified by the referee.
